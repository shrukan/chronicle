# Changelog

## [0.2.0](https://github.com/shrukan/chronicle/compare/v0.1.0...v0.2.0) (2026-10-07)


### Features

* Undo your last choice from the pause menu
* When the storybook is passed to one player, a full-screen notice covers the page until that player is ready, so secrets stay secret
* The screen stays on while the storybook is open
* Chronicle is published as a web app on GitHub Pages and as a container image for self-hosting

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-07)

The first release: *The Cost of Disease* from setup to the endings.


### Features

* The complete storybook of *The Cost of Disease*: setup, story, location pages, end of round, secret bids and votes, final scoring and the endings, with the original art, music and voice-over
* Works offline and can be added to the home screen; a game picks up again after a reload
* Pages open in one piece instead of "click to continue" after every paragraph
* Instructions for the app itself, like handing the storybook to a player, are shown as a separate notice
* Log book, play time and a gallery of the endings you have reached
* Click sound and the original's ending music
* Enter and Space press the main button on every screen
* About page with version, credits and licences; "Report a bug" links that fill in the details
* Final scoring includes the fifth player (the original app left them out)


### Bug Fixes

* Dialogs no longer open on top of each other
* The main menu fits short windows without scrolling
